Dipi Jethwa

Founder & Corporate Lawyer

Dipi Jethwa is a corporate and transactional lawyer with over a decade of experience advising on private equity and venture capital investments, M&A, corporate restructuring, commercial contracts, and regulatory compliance.

Enrolled as an Advocate with the Bar Council of Delhi, she has previously worked with leading Indian law firms, advising domestic and international clients across a wide range of complex transactions.

Her practice combines deep transactional expertise with hands-on execution, delivering solution-oriented legal support.
